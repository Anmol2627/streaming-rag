# Doc_21
### Visa & Documentation Support Policy

## §1 Purpose
This document describes company support available to employees requiring visa sponsorship or travel documentation for international business travel.

## §2 Business Visa Sponsorship Support
For destinations requiring a business visa, the company's travel desk provides an invitation letter and supporting documentation upon request, submitted at least 20 business days before the planned travel date.

## §3 Documentation Checklist
Employees requesting visa support must submit a valid passport copy, the approved travel request, and the host-country visa application form to the travel desk.

## §4 Processing Time
Standard visa documentation support requests are processed by the travel desk within 5 business days; expedited requests for travel within 20 business days are handled on a best-effort basis only.
