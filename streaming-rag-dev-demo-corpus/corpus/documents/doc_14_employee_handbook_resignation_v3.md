# Doc_14
### Employee Handbook — Resignation & Notice Period (v3)

## §1 Purpose
This section describes the standard resignation and notice period process applicable to all full-time employees, as maintained in version 3 of the Employee Handbook.

## §2 Standard Notice Period
The standard notice period for resignation is 30 days from the date written resignation is submitted through the HR portal, applicable to all full-time employees regardless of role or level.

## §3 Exit Interview Process
HR schedules an exit interview during the final week of the notice period to gather feedback on the employee's experience.

## §4 Final Settlement Timeline
Final settlement, including any pending reimbursements and leave encashment, is processed within 45 days of the employee's last working day.

## §5 Handover Requirements
Departing employees are required to complete a documented handover of active projects and access credentials to their manager before their last working day.
