# Doc_04
### General Event & Venue Booking Policy

## §1 Purpose & Scope
This policy governs the booking, modification, and cancellation of any company-operated or preferred-vendor event venue, including Grand Hall Pune, Riverside Conference Center Pune, and the Bangalore Tech Park Convention Wing.

## §2 Booking Lead Time Requirements
Minimum lead times are venue-specific and documented in each venue's own listing; this policy sets the floor at 3 business days for any venue booking regardless of size.

## §3 Deposit Requirements
All venue bookings require a refundable deposit as specified in the individual venue listing. Deposits are refunded within 10 business days following the event, provided no damage or cleaning-fee deductions apply.

## §4 Approval Workflow
Bookings with a total estimated cost (venue plus catering) exceeding INR 50,000 require budget-owner approval prior to confirmation. Bookings below this threshold may be confirmed directly by the requesting employee.

## §5 Cancellation & Modification Policy
For all venues covered by this policy, cancellations made more than 14 days before the event date receive a full refund of the deposit. Cancellations made within 14 days of the event date forfeit 50% of the deposit. Modifications to attendee count or date that do not change the venue selection may be made free of charge up to 5 business days before the event.

## §6 Force Majeure
In the event of circumstances beyond reasonable control (natural disaster, government-mandated closure, venue facility failure), deposits are refunded in full regardless of the timing of cancellation.
