### HR Policy Update Memo (2026) — Notice Period Revision

## §1 Purpose of This Update
This memo revises the standard resignation notice period described in the Employee Handbook, effective immediately upon publication.

## §2 Revised Notice Period
Effective immediately, the standard notice period for resignation is revised to 45 days from the date written resignation is submitted, superseding the 30-day period previously stated in Section 2 of the Employee Handbook.

## §3 Roles Affected
This revision applies to all full-time employees company-wide; no role or level is exempted from the revised notice period.

## §4 Effective Date
This memo is effective as of its publication date and applies to any resignation submitted on or after that date, regardless of the employee's original hire date.
