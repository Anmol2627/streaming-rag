# Doc_20
### Office Facilities Policy — Pune Campus

## §1 Overview
This document describes on-site facilities available to employees at the Pune campus.

## §2 Parking Allocation
On-site parking accommodates up to 80 vehicles, allocated on a first-come basis; two-wheeler parking is available separately with no capacity limit.

## §3 Cafeteria Hours
The campus cafeteria operates from 8:30 AM to 6:30 PM on weekdays; no meal subsidy applies at this location, unlike the Bangalore campus.

## §4 Meeting Room Booking
Meeting rooms at the Pune campus are booked through the internal calendar system; rooms seating more than 10 people require at least 24 hours advance notice.

## §5 Wellness Room & Fitness Facility
The Pune campus includes a small on-site fitness room, open from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM daily, equipped with cardio machines and free weights, accessible to all employees via badge entry with no separate membership fee.

## §6 Visitor Access Procedure
All visitors must be pre-registered by their host employee at least 2 hours in advance and must present a government-issued photo ID at the security desk.
