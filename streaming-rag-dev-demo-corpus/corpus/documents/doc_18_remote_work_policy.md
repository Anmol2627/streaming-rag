# Doc_18
### Remote Work Policy

## §1 Purpose & Philosophy
The company supports flexible work arrangements where role responsibilities allow, recognizing that sustained focus and collaboration can happen effectively outside a traditional office setting when supported by clear expectations and regular team connection.

## §2 Eligibility Criteria
Roles are eligible for remote work arrangements when the employee's core responsibilities do not require regular physical presence at a company facility, as determined jointly by the employee and their manager.

## §3 Core Collaboration Hours
Remote employees are expected to be reachable during core collaboration hours of 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time, regardless of their broader working schedule.

## §4 Home Office Equipment Stipend
Employees approved for a permanent remote arrangement receive a one-time home office equipment stipend of INR 15,000 toward a desk, chair, or monitor.

## §5 Co-Working Space Reimbursement
Employees who prefer a co-working environment over a home office may claim reimbursement of up to INR 5,000 per month toward a co-working space membership, subject to manager approval.

## §6 Manager Approval Process
Remote work arrangements are approved on a role-by-role basis by the employee's direct manager and reviewed at least annually.

## §7 Periodic Review of Remote Arrangements
Approved remote arrangements are reviewed every 12 months to confirm they remain appropriate for the employee's current role and team needs.
