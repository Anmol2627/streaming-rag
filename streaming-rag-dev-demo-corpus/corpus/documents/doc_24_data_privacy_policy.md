# Doc_24
### Data Privacy Policy

## §1 Purpose
This document describes how the company classifies, retains, and protects data collected in the course of business operations, including employee and event-attendee data.

## §2 Data Classification Levels
Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted; event attendee lists and expense records are classified as Confidential.

## §3 Data Retention Schedule
Confidential data, including expense records and attendee lists, is retained for 7 years from the date of creation, after which it is securely deleted unless a legal hold applies.

## §4 Third-Party Data Sharing Restrictions
Confidential or Restricted data may not be shared with a third-party vendor, including catering or venue partners, without a signed data processing agreement in place.

## §5 Employee Data Access Rights
Employees may request a copy of their own personnel data held by HR at any time by submitting a request through the internal HR portal; requests are fulfilled within 15 business days.

## §6 Breach Notification Procedure
Any suspected data breach must be reported to the IT Security team within 1 hour of discovery; affected individuals are notified within statutory timelines once the scope of the breach is confirmed.
