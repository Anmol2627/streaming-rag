# Doc_19
### Office Facilities Policy — Bangalore Campus

## §1 Overview
This document describes on-site facilities available to employees at the Bangalore Tech Park campus.

## §2 Parking Allocation
On-site parking is allocated on a first-come basis for the first 150 vehicles daily; overflow parking is available at a partner lot 400 meters from the main entrance.

## §3 Cafeteria Hours & Meal Subsidy
The campus cafeteria operates from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM on weekdays. Employees receive a meal subsidy of INR 150 per working day, automatically applied at the cafeteria point of sale.

## §4 Meeting Room Booking
Meeting rooms across the campus, excluding the Convention Wing (booked separately), are booked through the internal calendar system with no advance-notice requirement for rooms seating fewer than 8 people.

## §5 Mailroom & Courier Services
The campus mailroom processes incoming and outgoing courier requests between 9:00 AM and 6:00 PM on weekdays; same-day dispatch is available for requests submitted before 2:00 PM.

## §6 Visitor Access Procedure
All visitors must be pre-registered by their host employee at least 2 hours in advance and must present a government-issued photo ID at the security desk.

## §7 Lost & Found
Items found on campus are logged at the security desk and held for 30 days before being donated to a local charity partner.
