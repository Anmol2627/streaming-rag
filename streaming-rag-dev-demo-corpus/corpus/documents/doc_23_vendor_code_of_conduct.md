# Doc_23
### Vendor Code of Conduct

## §1 Purpose
This document establishes the minimum standards of conduct expected of all third-party vendors engaged by the company, including venue and catering partners.

## §2 Anti-Bribery Clause
Vendors must not offer, and employees must not accept, gifts or payments intended to influence a business decision; any such offer must be reported to the Procurement team immediately.

## §3 Confidentiality Requirements
Vendors with access to non-public company information, including event attendee lists, must sign a standard confidentiality agreement before engagement.

## §4 Termination of Vendor Relationship
The company reserves the right to terminate a vendor relationship with 30 days written notice, or immediately in the case of a confirmed code-of-conduct violation.

## §5 Reporting Violations
Suspected vendor violations of this code may be reported confidentially through the internal Ethics Hotline.
