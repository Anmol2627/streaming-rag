### Travel Policy Addendum Memo (2026)

## §1 Purpose of This Memo
This memo updates specific provisions of the Domestic and International Travel & Expense Policies effective for the dates specified below, pending formal incorporation into the next policy revision.

## §2 Updated Receipt Submission Window
Effective the dates in Section 4 of this memo, the receipt submission window for all travel expense claims, domestic and international, is reduced from 30 days to 15 days following trip completion.

## §3 Updated Director Approval Threshold
The late-booking exception threshold described in the International Travel & Expense Policy is unchanged by this memo and continues to apply as originally written.

## §4 Effective Date & Transition
This memo takes effect for all trips completed on or after the first day of the fiscal quarter following its publication; trips completed before that date remain subject to the original 30-day window.

## §5 Contact for Questions
Questions regarding this memo should be directed to the Finance policy team via the internal HR portal.
