# Doc_06
### Urban Bites Catering

## §1 Overview
Urban Bites is an approved catering vendor specializing in continental and fusion cuisine, based in Bangalore and primarily serving the Bangalore Tech Park Convention Wing.

## §2 Menu Packages
Two standard packages are offered: the Standard Package (sandwiches, salads, and beverages) and the Premium Package (a themed buffet with a rotating international menu).

## §3 International Cuisine Options
The Premium Package rotates weekly among Italian, Mexican, and Southeast Asian themes; the specific weekly theme must be confirmed with the vendor at time of booking.

## §4 Pricing Per Head
The Standard Package is priced at INR 350 per head. The Premium Package is priced at INR 700 per head, exclusive of applicable taxes.

## §5 Allergy Accommodation
Urban Bites maintains a standing list of allergen-free alternatives for shellfish, dairy, and gluten that can be requested at the time of order with no minimum notice period.

## §6 Equipment & Staffing
Urban Bites brings its own chafing dishes, serving staff, and disposable dinnerware; no additional equipment request is needed from the venue.

## §7 Lead Time Requirements
Standard Package orders require 3 business days notice; Premium Package orders require 7 business days notice due to themed-menu sourcing.
