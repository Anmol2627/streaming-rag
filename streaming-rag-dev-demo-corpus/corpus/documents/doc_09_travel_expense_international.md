# Doc_09
### International Travel & Expense Policy

## §1 Purpose & Scope
This policy governs reimbursable expenses for employees traveling internationally for business purposes and supplements, rather than replaces, the Domestic Travel & Expense Policy for any provisions not addressed here.

## §2 Additional Approval Requirements
All international travel requires pre-trip approval from the employee's department director, in addition to standard manager approval, prior to booking.

## §3 Foreign Currency Receipt Verification
Expense claims in a foreign currency must include the receipt in its original currency along with a bank-issued conversion statement; claims lacking a verifiable conversion statement will be processed at the exchange rate published by the Finance team on the date of the expense.

## §4 Late-Booking Exception Process
If international travel is booked less than 14 days before departure, the requesting employee's department director must provide written approval acknowledging the late booking before the trip is confirmed; standard manager approval alone is not sufficient in this case.

## §5 Visa & Documentation Costs
Visa application fees and required travel-document costs (passport renewal is excluded) are reimbursable in full with receipts.

## §6 Per Diem Rates (International)
The international meal per diem is USD 60 per day, disbursed in INR at the prevailing conversion rate at time of reimbursement.

## §7 Insurance Requirements
Employees traveling internationally on company business are automatically covered under the corporate travel insurance policy; no separate enrollment is required.

## §8 Reimbursement Timeline
International expense claims follow the same submission timeline as domestic claims unless otherwise updated by a subsequent policy memo.
