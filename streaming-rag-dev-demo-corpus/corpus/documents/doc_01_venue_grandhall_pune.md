# Doc_01
### Grand Hall Conference Center — Pune

## §1 Overview
Grand Hall Conference Center is a corporate event venue located in Pune's Koregaon Park business district, operated under a preferred-vendor agreement with the company's Events team. It is optimized for half-day and full-day workshops, training sessions, and client presentations.

## §2 Capacity & Layout
Grand Hall offers a single configurable room with a seated theatre-style capacity of 40 attendees, a classroom-style capacity of 32 attendees, and a boardroom-style capacity of 20 attendees. The maximum standing-reception capacity is 45 attendees. Requests exceeding 45 attendees cannot be accommodated at this venue regardless of layout.

## §3 Pricing & Deposit
The half-day rate is INR 18,000 and the full-day rate is INR 30,000, inclusive of standard AV equipment. A refundable deposit of INR 10,000 is required at the time of booking confirmation.

## §4 AV & Equipment
The venue includes a 120-inch projector screen, a wireless microphone set for up to two speakers, and a standard PA system. HDMI and USB-C adapters are provided on request; specialized recording equipment is not available on-site.

## §5 Catering Coordination Process
Grand Hall does not operate an in-house kitchen. External catering vendors from the company's approved list may be brought in with 48 hours advance notice to the venue coordinator for kitchen-access scheduling.

## §6 Accessibility Features
The venue is wheelchair accessible via a ground-floor entrance and includes an accessible restroom. There is no on-site elevator, so any breakout sessions must remain on the ground floor.

## §7 Booking Lead Time
Bookings must be requested at least 5 business days in advance for half-day sessions and 10 business days in advance for full-day sessions to guarantee availability.

## §8 Contact & Location
Grand Hall Conference Center, 2nd Cross Road, Koregaon Park, Pune. Venue coordinator bookings are handled through the internal Events Booking Portal.
