# Doc_10
### Expense Approval Workflow

## §1 Purpose
This document describes the internal routing and turnaround expectations for expense claims once submitted, across both domestic and international travel.

## §2 Approval Chain by Amount
Claims under INR 20,000 route to the direct manager only. Claims from INR 20,000 to INR 100,000 route to the direct manager and then Finance. Claims above INR 100,000 additionally require department-director sign-off.

## §3 Standard Turnaround Time
Manager-level approval is expected within 3 business days of submission; Finance-level review is expected within 5 additional business days.

## §4 Escalation Process
If a claim remains unapproved after 10 business days with no manager action, the employee may escalate directly to the Finance team's shared inbox for manual review.

## §5 Rejected Claims Process
Rejected claims include a written reason and may be resubmitted once with corrected documentation within 15 days of the rejection notice.

## §6 Recordkeeping Requirements
Approved claims and their supporting receipts are retained by Finance for 7 years in accordance with statutory audit requirements.
