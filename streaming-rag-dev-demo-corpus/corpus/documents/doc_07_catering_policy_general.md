# Doc_07
### General Catering Ordering Policy

## §1 Purpose
This policy governs the ordering, budgeting, and cancellation of catering services for company events, applicable regardless of which approved vendor is used.

## §2 Vendor Approval List
Only vendors on the company's approved list — currently Spice Route and Urban Bites — may be engaged using the corporate catering budget without a one-time exception approval from Procurement.

## §3 Ordering Lead Time
This policy sets a floor requirement of at least 3 business days notice for any catering order, independent of any additional lead time required by the individual vendor.

## §4 Allergy Disclosure Requirement
The event organizer is responsible for collecting attendee allergy and dietary information and disclosing it to the caterer no later than the vendor's stated notice window.

## §5 Budget Caps Per Event Type
Internal team meetings are capped at INR 400 per head; client-facing events are capped at INR 800 per head; exceptions require budget-owner sign-off.

## §6 Cancellation of Catering Orders
Catering orders cancelled more than 5 business days before the event incur no charge. Orders cancelled within 5 business days are billed at 50% of the order value.
