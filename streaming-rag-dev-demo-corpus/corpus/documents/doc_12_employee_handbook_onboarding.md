# Doc_12
### Employee Handbook — Onboarding

## §1 Welcome & Purpose
This section of the Employee Handbook outlines the standard onboarding process for all new full-time hires across company locations.

## §2 First Week Checklist
New hires complete ID badge issuance, workstation setup, and payroll enrollment within their first three working days, coordinated by the HR onboarding team.

## §3 IT Security Training Requirement
All new employees must complete the mandatory IT security awareness training module within their first 5 working days of employment; access to internal systems beyond email is restricted until this training is marked complete.

## §4 Buddy Program
Each new hire is paired with a peer buddy from their team for the first 30 days to assist with informal onboarding questions.

## §5 Benefits Enrollment Window
New hires have a 30-day window from their start date to enroll in optional benefits programs, including supplemental health coverage.

## §6 Workstation Setup
Laptop and peripheral issuance is coordinated by IT and typically completed before the employee's start date, provided onboarding paperwork is submitted at least 5 business days in advance.

## §7 Probation Period
All new hires serve a 90-day probation period, during which either party may end employment with 7 days' notice rather than the standard notice period.
