# Doc_05
### Spice Route Catering

## §1 Overview
Spice Route is an approved catering vendor specializing in North and South Indian cuisine, serving both Pune venues and available on request for Bangalore events with advance notice.

## §2 Menu Packages
Three standard packages are offered: Package A (tea/coffee and light snacks), Package B (working lunch with 2 main courses and rice/bread), and Package C (full multi-course lunch or dinner with dessert).

## §3 Vegetarian & Vegan Options
All three packages are available in a fully vegetarian variant at no additional cost. A vegan variant of Package B and Package C is available with 72 hours advance notice.

## §4 Pricing Per Head
Package A is priced at INR 250 per head, Package B at INR 450 per head, and Package C at INR 650 per head, exclusive of applicable taxes.

## §5 Allergy & Dietary Accommodation
Spice Route can accommodate nut, dairy, and gluten allergies when disclosed at least 5 business days before the event; accommodations requested with less notice cannot be guaranteed.

## §6 Minimum Order Size
A minimum order of 15 attendees applies to all packages. Orders below this threshold are billed at the 15-attendee rate.

## §7 Service Area Coverage
Spice Route provides on-site staffing and setup for Grand Hall and Riverside Conference Center in Pune at no additional travel charge; a travel surcharge of INR 3,000 applies for events in Bangalore.
