# Doc_08
### Domestic Travel & Expense Policy

## §1 Purpose & Scope
This policy governs reimbursable travel expenses for employees traveling within the country for business purposes.

## §2 Eligible Expense Categories
Eligible categories include airfare or train fare, ground transportation, lodging, and meals, each subject to the caps described in this policy.

## §3 Reimbursement Submission Timeline
Employees must submit expense claims with itemized receipts within 30 days of trip completion. Claims submitted after this window require manager exception approval and may be denied at the manager's discretion.

## §4 Receipt Requirements
Original itemized receipts are required for any single expense exceeding INR 500. Expenses below this threshold may be claimed with a self-certified declaration.

## §5 Per Diem Rates
The domestic meal per diem is INR 1,200 per day for metro cities and INR 900 per day for non-metro cities.

## §6 Approval Thresholds
Claims under INR 20,000 require only direct-manager approval. Claims of INR 20,000 or more require additional finance-team review before disbursement.

## §7 Advance Booking Requirements
Airfare and lodging should be booked at least 7 days in advance where possible; late bookings made within 7 days of travel require a brief justification note attached to the expense claim but do not require special approval.

## §8 Non-Reimbursable Items
Alcohol, personal entertainment, and traffic or parking fines incurred during business travel are not reimbursable under any circumstance.
