# Doc_13
### Employee Handbook — Leave Policy

## §1 Purpose & Scope
This section describes paid and unpaid leave entitlements applicable to all full-time employees.

## §2 Casual Leave
Employees accrue 12 days of casual leave per calendar year, usable for short-notice personal matters, with a maximum of 3 consecutive days per request without additional documentation.

## §3 Sick Leave
Employees accrue 10 days of sick leave per calendar year. Absences beyond 3 consecutive sick days require a medical certificate.

## §4 Earned/Privilege Leave
Employees accrue 18 days of earned leave per calendar year, applied for at least 5 business days in advance except in emergencies.

## §5 Maternity & Paternity Leave
Maternity leave is provided for 26 weeks in accordance with statutory requirements. Paternity leave is provided for 2 weeks, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth.

## §6 Leave Carry-Forward Rules
Up to 10 days of unused earned leave may be carried forward into the following calendar year; casual and sick leave do not carry forward.

## §7 Leave Application Process
All leave requests are submitted through the internal HR portal and require direct-manager approval before the leave is confirmed as taken.

## §8 Public Holidays
The company observes 10 public holidays per calendar year, published annually by HR at the start of the fiscal year.
