# Doc_03
### Bangalore Tech Park Convention Wing

## §1 Overview
The Convention Wing at Bangalore Tech Park is the company's primary large-scale event venue in Bangalore, used for all-hands meetings, hackathons, and multi-team offsites.

## §2 Room Codes & Capacity
Room B-204 seats 100 attendees theatre-style. Room B-210 seats 60 attendees classroom-style. Room B-215 is a boardroom seating 16. Combined room B-204 and B-210 (partition removed) accommodates up to 180 attendees standing-reception style.

## §3 Pricing & Deposit
Internal department bookings are billed via cost-center chargeback rather than a direct invoice; a placeholder deposit of INR 15,000 is held against the requesting cost center for damage or cleaning contingencies.

## §4 Technology Infrastructure
Room B-204 includes a dual-projector setup, a 12-microphone conference array, and dedicated hackathon-grade Wi-Fi capable of supporting 200 simultaneous connections. Rooms B-210 and B-215 include standard single-projector AV.

## §5 Ambience & Design Notes
The Convention Wing features an open, glass-walled design with natural light throughout the day, exposed-brick interior accents, and a landscaped atrium adjoining Room B-204 that is frequently used for informal networking breaks between sessions.

## §6 Nearby Accommodation
Two partner hotels within walking distance (Hotel Meridian Bangalore and Park View Suites) offer negotiated corporate rates for out-of-town attendees; booking codes are available from the venue coordinator.

## §7 Booking Lead Time
Room B-204 and combined-room bookings require 15 business days advance notice due to high demand. Room B-210 and B-215 require 5 business days advance notice.

## §8 Contact & Location
Bangalore Tech Park, Whitefield Main Road, Bangalore. Convention Wing bookings are handled through the internal Events Booking Portal.
