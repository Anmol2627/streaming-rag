### Internal Meeting Room Booking Policy (General)

## §1 Purpose
This policy governs the booking of standard internal meeting rooms, as distinct from large-format event venues covered under the Event & Venue Booking Policy.

## §2 Booking Channels
Standard meeting rooms are booked directly through the internal calendar system's room-booking integration; no separate approval is required for rooms seating fewer than 12 people.

## §3 Priority Rules
Client-facing meetings take booking priority over internal-only meetings when a room conflict arises within 24 hours of the meeting time.

## §4 No-Show Policy
A room booking is automatically released 15 minutes after its scheduled start time if no check-in occurs via the room's calendar display panel.

## §5 Equipment Requests
Standard rooms include a display screen and conference phone; requests for additional equipment (webcams, wireless presentation adapters) are submitted to IT at least 1 business day in advance.
