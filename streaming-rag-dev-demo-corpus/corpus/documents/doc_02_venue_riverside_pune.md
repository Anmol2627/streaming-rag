# Doc_02
### Riverside Conference Center — Pune

## §1 Overview
Riverside Conference Center is a larger-format event venue located along Mula-Mutha Riverfront, Pune, suited to workshops, town halls, and multi-day training programs requiring breakout space.

## §2 Capacity & Layout
Riverside offers a main hall with a theatre-style capacity of 80 attendees and two adjoining breakout rooms each seating 20 attendees classroom-style. The maximum standing-reception capacity across the main hall is 100 attendees.

## §3 Pricing & Deposit
The full-day rate for the main hall is INR 55,000; each breakout room is an additional INR 8,000 per day. A refundable deposit of INR 20,000 is required at booking confirmation.

## §4 Parking & Access
On-site parking accommodates up to 40 vehicles. Overflow parking is available at a public lot located 300 meters from the venue entrance.

## §5 Outdoor Event Space
An adjoining riverside lawn area is available for outdoor receptions for up to 150 guests, subject to a separate outdoor-use permit processed by the venue coordinator at least 15 business days in advance.

## §6 Booking Process
Requests are submitted through the internal Events Booking Portal and require sign-off from the requesting department's budget owner for bookings exceeding INR 40,000.

## §7 Contact & Location
Riverside Conference Center, Riverfront Road, Pune. Venue coordinator bookings are handled through the internal Events Booking Portal.
